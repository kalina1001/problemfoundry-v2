04 / EXAMPLE REVIEW

What a workflow review
looks at.

Illustrative example — not a client case. A supplier receives orders by email and re-enters the details into a spreadsheet.

TODAY

How the work happens

Email arrives → details are copied to a spreadsheet → stock is checked with a colleague → a reply is written manually.

REVIEW

Where effort may be lost

Repeated data entry, waiting for stock information, unclear ownership and corrections when order details are missed.

POSSIBLE PLAN

What to consider changing

Use one order intake format, assign a clear status owner and simplify the stock check. Automate only where current tools support it.

The actual findings and recommendations depend on the team’s process, tools and constraints.
